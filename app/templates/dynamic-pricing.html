Fair questions

Will it undercut my rates? +
It cannot go below the floor you set, on any room type. You decide the lowest and highest price. It works only inside that.
Do I have to let it change prices on its own? +
Not at all. Most hotels start in approval mode — you see the suggestion, you tap yes. Move to automatic only if and when you want to.
What if I've already fixed my Diwali rate? +
Lock that date. Smart Pricing skips it completely — no suggestion, no change.
Why should I trust the number it picks? +
Tap any date and it explains itself in plain words: how full you are, how that compares to last year, which festival is near, how soon the date is. Every price has a reason you can read.
Is my current setup at risk? +
No. It runs alongside everything you use today, and stays off until you switch it on. Switch it back off and your rates stay exactly where they are.
How long before I see something? +
Your first suggestions appear within a few hours of switching it on.
