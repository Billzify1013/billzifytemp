Domestic Devotion Meets International Tourism

Amritsar draws two quite different crowds at once. Pilgrims and domestic visitors come for the Golden Temple in enormous numbers, booking mostly through Indian platforms and often at short notice. At the same time, Amritsar is firmly on the international tourist trail, with foreign visitors booking months ahead through global platforms.

For a hotelier, that means your rooms are being sold across a wider spread of platforms than in most Indian cities — domestic and international, short-notice and long-lead, all against the same inventory. Every additional platform is another place your availability can drift out of date, and Amritsar's peak periods around Baisakhi, Gurpurab, and the tourist season leave no room for that drift.
