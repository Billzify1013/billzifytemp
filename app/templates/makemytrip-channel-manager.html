MakeMyTrip Channel Manager for Indian Hotels

MakeMyTrip is one of the biggest sources of hotel bookings in India — which means it is also the platform most likely to sell a room you have already sold somewhere else. A channel manager keeps your MakeMyTrip inventory in step with every other platform, automatically.

Why MakeMyTrip Matters for Indian Hotels

MakeMyTrip is among the most widely used travel platforms in India, and for a large number of Indian properties it is the single biggest source of online bookings. Domestic travellers — families, pilgrims, business visitors, weekend tourists — search and book there in enormous numbers.

That reach is exactly why it deserves careful handling. A platform that brings you the most bookings is also the platform that can do the most damage when your availability there is wrong.

The Problem: MakeMyTrip Is Never Your Only Platform

Almost no hotel sells on MakeMyTrip alone. Most Indian properties also list on other booking platforms and take direct bookings, walk-ins, and phone reservations. That means the same rooms are on sale in several places at the same time.

Here is where it breaks. A guest books your last available room on another platform at 10pm. On MakeMyTrip, that room is still showing as available — and it will keep showing as available until someone manually logs in and closes it. Anyone booking in that window gets a confirmation for a room that no longer exists.

The reverse happens too. A MakeMyTrip booking arrives, and your other platforms keep selling the same room until you update them. The gap between the sale and the update is where every double booking is born.

What Goes Wrong When MakeMyTrip Is Out of Sync

  • Double bookings — two guests, one room, and one of them turned away at the door.
  • Refunds and relocation — you return the money and often pay to move the guest elsewhere.
  • Reviews you cannot undo — a guest denied a confirmed room writes the kind of review that stays visible for months.
  • Rooms sitting unsold — the quieter loss. To feel safe, hoteliers hold rooms back or forget to reopen them after a cancellation, and those genuinely empty rooms never get booked.
  • Rate mismatches — different prices showing on different platforms confuses guests and undermines trust.

How a Channel Manager Fixes It

A channel manager holds one master copy of your rooms, rates and availability, and keeps every connected platform reading from it — MakeMyTrip included.

You set your inventory and pricing in one place instead of logging into separate extranets. When a booking comes in from any source, that room is closed everywhere at once. When a cancellation frees a room, it reopens everywhere at once. There is no window left for a clash to happen.

In practice this means you stop managing MakeMyTrip as a separate job. It simply becomes one of the platforms your single dashboard controls — always current, without you touching it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a MakeMyTrip channel manager? +
It is software that keeps your hotel's availability and rates on MakeMyTrip synchronised with every other platform you sell on. When a room sells anywhere, it is closed on MakeMyTrip automatically, and when a room sells on MakeMyTrip it is closed everywhere else.
Why do I need one if MakeMyTrip brings me most bookings? +
Precisely because it does. The more bookings a platform sends you, the more often your availability changes, and the more likely a manual delay causes a double booking. High-volume platforms need accurate inventory the most.
Can I manage MakeMyTrip rates without logging into it separately? +
That is one of the main benefits. With a channel manager you set your rates and availability centrally, so you are not opening a separate extranet every time something changes.
Will this stop double bookings on MakeMyTrip? +
It removes the cause. Double bookings happen because of the delay between a room selling and being closed elsewhere. Real-time sync removes that delay. The one thing you must also do is record walk-in and phone bookings in the system so it knows about them.
