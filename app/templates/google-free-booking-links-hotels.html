Google Free Booking Links for Hotels: Direct Bookings, Zero Commission

Google now lets hotels show their own booking link on their listing, free of charge. For a hotel paying commission on every OTA reservation, this is one of the most valuable things you can set up — and most Indian properties still haven't.

What Are Google's Free Booking Links?

When a traveller looks at a hotel on Google, they see a set of booking options with prices. Historically every one of those belonged to an OTA. Google has since opened that space so that hotels can show their own booking link there too, without paying Google for the placement.

For a hotel, this is a rare thing: a position in front of ready-to-book travellers that does not cost a commission on every reservation. The traveller clicks your link, lands on your own booking page, and books with you.

What This Actually Saves You

Think about the journey of a single booking. A traveller searches, sees your property, compares prices, and books. If they book through an OTA link, a share of that room rate leaves your business permanently. If they book through your own link, it does not.

Now multiply that by every booking that originates from Google — which for most hotels is a substantial slice of their online business. The difference is not marginal. It is the gap between a booking that funds your property and a booking that funds a platform.

And crucially, this is not new demand you have to go out and create. It is demand that already exists, already searching for you, already deciding. You are simply choosing to receive it directly.

What You Need for It to Work

  • Live rates sent to Google — your prices and availability, delivered in the format Google requires and kept current.
  • A booking page that works — travellers who click need to be able to complete the booking on your own site.
  • Accurate inventory — Google's availability must match every other platform you sell on, or you will double-book.
  • A complete Google Business Profile — your property's listing needs to be properly set up and verified.
  • Ongoing maintenance — a feed that stops updating stops working.

The Reason Most Hotels Miss This

None of the requirements above are commercially difficult. All of them are technically difficult if you do not have a developer.

That single fact explains why so much of this opportunity goes unused across Indian hospitality. OTAs have engineering teams whose job is exactly this. An independent hotel has an owner who is already doing five jobs. So the OTA links appear on the listing, and the hotel's do not.

The imbalance is not about who deserves the booking. It is about who had the technical capacity to claim the space.
