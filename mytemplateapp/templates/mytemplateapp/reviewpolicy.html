Privacy Policy

Effective from 5 November 2026

This policy explains how Billzify Technology India ("Billzify", "we", "us") handles information when businesses use Billzify Review AI (the "Service"), available at billzify.com. By using the Service you agree to this policy.

1. Who this applies to

The Service is used by business owners and their authorised staff ("you"). It also processes limited information about your customers, such as the name and phone number on a bill, and the public reviews they post on Google.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, business name, phone number, email address and billing details for your subscription.
  • Customer contact details: customer name and mobile number from bills created in Billzify, used only to send that customer your bill and your review link.
  • Google Business Profile data: described in section 3.
  • Usage data: log-in times, actions taken in the dashboard (for example, approving a reply) and basic device and browser information, used for security and support.

3. Google Business Profile data

When you choose to connect your Google Business Profile, you sign in with Google and grant the Service access using the business.manage permission, plus the basic openid and email permissions. With that access we:

  • read your Google account email address, only to show you which Google account is connected;
  • read the list of business locations on your account (name, address and Maps link) so you can pick which ones to connect;
  • read reviews and existing review replies for the locations you connect;
  • post, update or remove a reply to a review, either after you or your authorised staff approve it in the dashboard, or automatically for the reviews covered by auto-reply if you have turned that setting on;
  • read the performance numbers Google provides for the locations you connect: how many times the profile was seen on Google Search and Maps, and how many people called, visited the website, asked for directions, sent a message, made a booking or viewed the menu, by day;
  • read the monthly list of search words people used before they saw your business, as Google provides it;
  • check whether a review you have told us you reported to Google is still on Google, so we can show you when it has been removed.

We do not use this access to create, post or report reviews, to edit your business information, hours, photos or posts, or to access locations you have not connected. Reporting a review is done by you on Google's own page; Billzify only records that you reported it.

Billzify Review AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the review features you see in the Service. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties except as needed to run the Service (see section 6) or when required by law.

4. How we use information

  • To show your reviews and help you draft and post replies.
  • To show growth reports: profile views, calls, website visits, directions and search words, and how they change over time.
  • To track reviews you have reported to Google and show you when Google removes them.
  • To send your review link to customers along with their bill.
  • To send you alerts and monthly reports about your reviews.
  • To provide support, keep the Service secure and prevent misuse.
  • To process your subscription payments.

5. AI-drafted replies

To draft a reply, the text of a review, the reviewer's first name, the star rating, your business name and your chosen reply style are sent to an AI language-model provider (such as Google Gemini) that processes it on our behalf. Performance numbers and your Google account details are never sent to the AI provider. The provider may not use this data to train its models. By default a draft is never posted to Google until a person approves it. If you turn on auto-reply, drafts for the reviews you selected (for example, 4–5 star reviews) are posted automatically. Auto-reply is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.

6. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Service, such as cloud hosting, SMS and WhatsApp messaging providers, payment processors and the AI provider mentioned above. They act on our instructions and must keep the data confidential. We may also disclose information if required by law. We do not sell personal data.

7. Storage and security

Data is stored on secure cloud servers. Google access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and are used only for the account that granted them. Access to production data is limited to staff who need it to operate the Service.

8. Retention and deletion

  • When you disconnect Google from Billzify settings, or revoke access from your Google Account permissions page, we delete the stored Google tokens and stop all access. Cached review data for that account is deleted within 30 days.
  • When you close your Billzify account, we delete your account data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example, tax invoices).
  • Replies you have already posted remain on Google and can be removed by you from your Business Profile.

9. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by writing to us at the address below. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may also raise a grievance with us and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

The Service is meant for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the dashboard or by email.
